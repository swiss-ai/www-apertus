### Contributing to the Apertus website

Thank you for considering contributing to this repository! We welcome your participation in building this project.

Please note that we have an audience at least as diverse as our maintainer pool: the [Contributor Covenant](CONDUCT.md) applies as a basic Code of Conduct for our community.

## For a varied audience

The website contains a basic introduction for a general audience (`content`), instructions for getting started (`content/pages`) for people with some IT proficiency, as well as a documentation area (`content/docs`) with details for experts. Community contributions to all of these areas are encouraged.

While the news area (`content/articles`) is dedicated primarily to announcements from the project team, we also are happy to report on benchmarking and implementation efforts. Please check with us first before writing an article in collaboration with our communications/community leads.

## AI and LLM Usage Policy

If your contribution appears generated by LLM or AI tools, **and** you attempt to obfuscate that, it will be closed and you are likely to be blocked.

Perfection is never required. Your own words and abilities are enough. Do not use LLM tools to only translate or "improve" what you say and do.

You must carefully consider social, environmental, and quality issues with LLMs before deciding to use them.

## Ethical and Legal Policy

In addition to these policies, please follow the guidelines outlined in the [Apertus Charter](content/pages/charter.md) and [Acceptable Use Policy](content/pages/legal.md).
